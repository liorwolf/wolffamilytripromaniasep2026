# Plan review: holes in the Sep 16-22 plan, and alternatives with more hiking and less driving

Reviewed 10 Sep 2026 against the itinerary PDF (see [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/__original\|__original]]). Verified facts are marked with the source at the bottom. Sunset in the mountains is about 19:15 on 18-21 Sep, sunrise about 07:00. Late September at 2000 m can be 0-10 C with wind; pack layers, hat, gloves, rain shell.

## Holes, day by day

### Wed 16 Sep, arrival
- Landing 21:10, passport, luggage, car pick-up: at the hotel about 23:00. Fine. Check the rental desk is open at 22:00, otherwise collect the car in the morning.
- The hotel is Hilton Garden Inn Bucharest Airport (there is no plain "Hilton airport").

### Thu 17 Sep, Sinaia and Peleș
- Airport to Sinaia is 2 to 2.5 hours on DN1, more if leaving after 09:00.
- Peleș is open Thursday 09:15-16:00, last entry 16:00, 2,000 visitors a day, closed Monday and Tuesday. Book online at bilete.peles.ro before the trip. Thursday is the right day.
- "Franz Joseph path, 24 min drive": both readings are right. The Royal Path (Poteca Regală) is the walk from the Peleș car park up to Poiana Stânii Regale and the Franz Joseph cliffs, 4 km and 490 m each way, about 1 h 10 up. There is also a gravel forest road from the Cota 1400 road to the meadow, about 23 min by car, passable in a normal car when dry (Aya checked, 12 Sep 2026). See alternative A and [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Day 2 - Thu Sep 17 - Bucharest to Sinaia, Peleș Castle\|Day 2 - Thu Sep 17 - Bucharest to Sinaia, Peleș Castle]].

### Fri 18 Sep, Bucegi
- Gondola Sinaia runs Tuesday to Friday 08:30-16:00, the upper section (Cota 1400 to 2000) 09:30-16:30. Friday is fine.
- "Peak of mountain then 1:20 hours walk to Sphinx" is wrong. From Cota 2000 it is 1.5 to 2 hours to Piatra Arsă and another hour to Babele and the Sphinx, so 2.5 to 3 hours each way. A there-and-back to the Sphinx does not fit the 16:30 last gondola down. The fix is to come down the other side by the Bușteni cable car (last descent 17:00) and take the train or a taxi back to Sinaia. See alternative B.
- The lake option (Bolboci, Cheile Tătarului) is a 40 min drive up the Transbucegi road (narrow, very winding). The Sphinx is not near the lake: from there you drive on to Piatra Arsă and walk 1 hour. It is a driving day with short walks, and the same road is used again on Sep 19 if you go to the waterfall.

### Sat 19 Sep, Sinaia to Rasnov
- Cascada Șapte Izvoare is in the Ialomița valley, reached via Cheile Zănoagei and Lake Scropoasa, 1 hour drive in the same direction as Bolboci, then a 2 to 3 hour walk. Then back through Sinaia to Rasnov: 3 hours of driving for a waterfall the plan itself doubts. Drop it. See alternative C.
- Hotel Sorin MIHAI is in Cristian, between Rasnov and Brasov (Strada Lungă 215), not in Rasnov itself. Free parking, restaurant. Drive times in the plan are about right from there.

### Sun 20 Sep, Rasnov and Bran area
- Five options plus bear watching is two days of activity. Pick two.
- Bran castle on a Sunday is the second-busiest day of the week. Be at the gate at 09:00 opening or after 16:00. Ticket about 120 lei (€24).
- Devil's Mill waterfall (Cascada Moara Dracului) is not a 41 min drive to a viewpoint. It is at 1,575 m in the Bucegi, reached by an 11 km, 3 to 3.5 hour hike from Șimon village near Bran. Good hike, wrong category in the plan.
- Rasnov fortress is closed for renovation until 2028. Only the grounds and the panorama are open.
- Bear watching via medveles.hu: their hides are in Harghita county, not near Brasov. The Băile Tușnad hide is about 1.5 hours from Cristian (69 km to Brasov plus 15 min), the Borzont hide near Gheorgheni is about 3 hours. Find out which one is booked. The program starts 2 hours before sunset, so about 17:15, ends about 19:45, and you are back at the hotel around 21:30. That is 3 hours of driving for the evening and it kills the afternoon. Alternative: bear hides at the edge of Zărnești, 15 min from the hotel, run by the local forest manager with tours from Brasov at sunset (GetYourGuide, Viator, Romanian Friend), about 85-100% sighting rate. Or the Libearty bear sanctuary in Zărnești in the morning (rescued bears, not wild, 1 hour walk, tours at 09:00, book 48 hours ahead, +40 268 471 202).

### Mon 21 Sep, Brasov area, then Bucharest
- Seven Ladders Canyon: 15 min from Brasov in Timișu de Jos, 30 min from Cristian. Park, walk 45 min up the forest road, then the canyon on metal ladders, about 2.5 hours round trip. Entry fee, zipline option. Guy and Noa will like it. It is on DN1 on the way from Sinaia, so it fits Sep 19 better. See alternative C.
- Tamina canyon: parking on DN1 at the Brasov end of Timișu de Sus, 1 hour 15 up to the waterfall, 2.5 to 3 hours in total. Same area as Seven Ladders, do one or the other.
- Zărnești Cliffs means Zărnești Gorge (Prăpăstiile Zărneștilor), the flat walk into Piatra Craiului. The real prize is above it, see alternative E.
- Cristian to Bucharest is 3 hours in normal traffic, 4 or more when DN1 is jammed. Monday evening is much better than Sunday. Leave by 15:00 to arrive at 18:00-18:30 with light left for the old town.
- Concorde Old Bucharest: parking must be reserved in advance, and the old town has pedestrian zones, so get the exact driving approach from the hotel.

### Tue 22 Sep, Bucharest and flight
- Leaving the old town at 17:45 for a 20:45 El Al flight means arriving at the airport about 18:40 after evening traffic and returning the rental car. El Al asks for 3 hours before departure and its security queue at Otopeni is slow. Tight. Leave at 17:00, or move Caru' cu Bere to 15:30, or make it a 13:00 lunch (see [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Last day options - Tue Sep 22 - me and Guy\|Last day options - Tue Sep 22 - me and Guy]] for the Therme version of the day).

## General
- The plan lists drive times but no walking times. Almost every stop is a hike of 1 to 4 hours; that is the budget that matters.
- No rest days and no buffer. If it rains in the Bucegi on Sep 18, the same plan can move to Sep 19.
- Two nights in two Sinaia hotels for one group is awkward but fine. Check breakfast times against early starts.

## Alternatives I would like more

### A. Thu 17 Sep: hike from the castle instead of driving
Peleș at 11:30 (booked), then walk straight from the car park up the Royal Path to Poiana Stânii Regale and the Franz Joseph cliffs. 8 km, 3 hours, viewpoint over the whole Prahova valley. Zero driving after the castle. Aya and Tal can stop at Poiana Stânii (a meadow with a terrace) or do the castles (Peleș plus Pelișor) and the town.

### B. Fri 18 Sep: the Bucegi traverse, the best day of the trip
Sinaia gondola at 08:30 (first car), Cota 1400, up to Cota 2000. Walk up Vârful Furnica (2,103 m, 30 min, views both ways), then the plateau trail to Piatra Arsă (1.5 hours), Babele and the Sphinx (1 hour), optional Caraiman Cross (1 hour more, the big cross above Bușteni, the best view over the valley). Down by the Bușteni cable car from Babele (last descent 17:00), train or taxi 10 minutes back to Sinaia. 12 to 15 km, 5 to 6 hours, almost all above 2,000 m with 360 degree views. Omu peak (2,505 m) is 2 to 2.5 hours more each way from Babele, only if everyone is strong and the first gondola is on time.
Aya and Tal: gondola to Cota 2000, short walk on the plateau, gondola down, afternoon in Sinaia.

### C. Sat 19 Sep: Bușteni and Seven Ladders on the way to Rasnov
Check out, 10 min to Bușteni, walk to Cascada Urlătoarea (45 min each way through forest, pretty, easy). If the traverse was rained out, do the Bușteni cable car to Babele and Caraiman Cross instead. Then drive DN1 over Predeal 40 min to Timișu de Jos, Seven Ladders Canyon (2.5 hours, ladders, zipline for Guy and Noa), then 30 min to Cristian. All stops are on the direct road, no backtracking.

### D. Sun 20 Sep: Bran early, a real hike, bears close by
Bran castle at 09:00 (1.5 hours). Then one of:
- Moara Dracului waterfall from Șimon, 11 km, 3 to 3.5 hours, quiet, wild, into the west side of the Bucegi.
- Măgura and Peștera villages on the plateau between Piatra Craiului and Bucegi: walk between the two villages (2 to 3 hours), old farms, haystacks, the Piatra Craiului wall on one side and Bucegi on the other. This also covers the "villages where people live the old way" line in the plan, with Viscri dropped (1:20 each way).
Evening: bear hide near Zărnești at sunset, 15 min away, home by 20:30. Brasov old town for dinner is 20 min the other way.

### E. Mon 21 Sep: Piatra Craiului, then Bucharest
Start 08:00 at Fântâna lui Botorog. Zărnești Gorge, up to Cabana Curmătura (1,470 m, 2 to 2.5 hours), on to Piatra Mică (1,816 m, 1 hour more) for the view along the Piatra Craiului ridge and across to Bucegi, back down the Zănoaga meadow loop. 12 km, about 1,000 m up and down, 5 to 6 hours, packed lunch or soup at the hut. Aya and Tal: the gorge only, flat, 1.5 hours, then the café at Botorog.
Back at the car by 14:30, Bucharest by 18:00.
Cheaper on legs: Tâmpa above Brasov old town, 1 hour up, or Postăvarul peak from Poiana Brașov by cable car and a 1 hour walk, then 2 hours in the Brasov old town before the drive.

### F. If the last night in Bucharest is negotiable
Sleep Mon 21 Sep in Bușteni or Sinaia instead (1 hour from Zărnești), drive to Bucharest Tuesday morning (2 hours, no evening traffic), old town from 11:00, dinner 15:30, airport 17:00. This turns the 3 hour Monday evening drive into a morning drive and gives a full Piatra Craiului day. Costs the Bucharest evening.
