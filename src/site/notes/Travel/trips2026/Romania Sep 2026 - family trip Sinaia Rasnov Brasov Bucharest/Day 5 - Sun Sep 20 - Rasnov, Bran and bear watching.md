# Day 5 - Sun Sep 20 - Rasnov, Bran and bear watching

The bear day, the village day. The **Libearty bear sanctuary** at Zărnești at 10:00 (Aya bought the tickets on 15 Sep) replaces the wild bear hide, which is off the plan. That gives back the whole afternoon: Bran castle **from the outside only** (Aya's choice), the Măgura plateau for a walk and lunch, then the Rucăr-Bran pass to Fundata and Șirnea, and dinner in Brasov old town. Nothing after the sanctuary is booked, so the afternoon can shrink or swap by mood and weather. The sanctuary is 20 min from the hotel, so we leave at 09:20, the only day with an earlier start than 10:00. Be at the gate 15 min before the tour.

## Stops in detail

### Libearty bear sanctuary, Zărnești (booked)
- **Worth it: 5/5.** Run by the charity Milioane de Prieteni (Millions of Friends): 69 hectares of oak forest at the edge of Zărnești holding about 100 brown bears rescued from cages, circuses and restaurants, plus a few wolves and deer. The Classic Tour is a guided walk of about 1 h 15 on a 2 km forest trail past the big fenced enclosures, with the bears a few metres away in the trees, pools and meadows. Not wild, but guaranteed bears, and the guide tells each animal's story.
- **Tickets: bought by Aya (15 Sep) on millionsoffriends.org for Sunday 20 Sep, 10:00 tour.** Prices that day: adult 100 lei (€20), student 80 lei (€16), child 55 lei (€11). Show the e-tickets on the phone at the gate. Tours run Tuesday to Sunday in the morning only, so the bears are not disturbed. Quiet voices on the trail, no feeding, no flash.
- Getting there: 20 min from the Cristian hotel via Râșnov to Zărnești, then the signposted road to the sanctuary gate (Strada Tohanul Vechi, free parking). Toilets and a small shop at the entrance. Shoes for a forest path.

### Bran castle, outside only
- **Worth it: 4/5 from outside, which is where it looks best.** The 14th-century castle sits on a rock above the village with its towers and red roofs; the famous photographs are all taken from below. Inside it is Queen Marie's furnished 1920s house, small rooms and a lot of stairs, and on a Sunday at noon it is packed. Skipping it saves 100 lei (€20) a head and an hour and a half.
- Where to look from: the meadow at the foot of the rock (walk through the souvenir market from the car park, 5 min), the path up to the gate (free up to the ticket booth), and, best of all, the open fields at the start of the Măgura road 300 m south of the castle, where you see the whole castle above the village with Bucegi behind. 20 min in total.
- If anyone changes their mind on the spot: tickets at the gate, about 100 lei (€20) adults, 40 lei (€8) for Tal (5-17), 60 lei (€12) students with a card; expect a 20-30 min queue at noon on a Sunday. Open until 18:00.

### Măgura and Peștera villages
- **Worth it: 5/5 for the setting, and this is the "villages where people still live the old way" line of the original plan, done properly.** Two scattered villages on a high plateau at 1,000 m between the Piatra Craiului wall and the Bucegi, wooden farms, cows, hay drying on poles, zero traffic. In late September the meadows are golden.
- Driving: from Bran centre take the road signposted Măgura (Strada Măgura), 6 km, gravel for the last part, fine in a normal car, 20 min. Park at the village church or by the pension you eat at.
- Walking: the marked path between Măgura and Peștera is 3 km each way on dirt tracks with 150 m of ups and downs; walk as far as you like and turn around, 1 h 15 is a good version. Wear proper shoes, there are cows and mud. Views the whole way.
- Peștera has a small cave (Peștera Liliecilor, the bat cave, with a chapel built into the entrance) 20 min on foot from the village if anyone wants a cave.
- Lunch: Pensiunea Casa Zimbrilor (terrace facing Piatra Craiului, home cooking) or Hanul Măgura; both need a Sunday booking. Cash safer.

#### For Lior during the Măgura hours: Bran to Măgura on foot, or the waterfall
- The family drives up to Măgura; you run there from Bran and meet them at the village. Trail marked with a yellow cross from the Vama Medievală Bran museum, opposite the castle car park: 8 km, 450 m of climbing on forest paths and dirt roads with views back onto the castle, 3-4 hours at a walking pace, 1 h 15 at yours. Leave Bran 12:00, in Măgura 13:15, join the end of the walk and lunch at 13:50.
- Bigger: the **Moara Dracului (Devil's Mill) waterfall** from Șimon village, 5 min from Bran: 11 km round trip, 500 m up, 3 to 3.5 hours walking, 1 h 45 running. Drop-off in Șimon at 11:50, back at 13:35, the family collects you on the way down from Măgura at 15:05. Wild and quiet, into the west face of the Bucegi; the waterfall at 1,575 m is small in September but the valley is the point. Sandwiches in the car instead of lunch.

### Rucăr-Bran pass, Fundata and Șirnea
- **Worth it: 5/5 for the views, 4/5 for the villages.** The old road from Transylvania to Wallachia climbs from Bran through Moieciu to the pass at 1,290 m and the village of Fundata at 1,360 m, the highest in Romania, with hayfields, wooden farms and the two big massifs on either side. Șirnea, on a side road, is a ridge of scattered farms that started Romanian village tourism in 1968; a walk along its road is 45 min of pure views. Farms sell smoked cheese and brânză de burduf (sheep cheese in fir bark).
- Driving: Bran to Fundata 25 min on DN73 (good asphalt), Fundata to Șirnea 10 min (narrow, partly gravel), back to Bran 30 min, then 25 min to Cristian.
- Cheile Grădiștei is a resort complex at Fundata with a restaurant terrace, horse riding, carriage rides and ATVs; a good coffee stop, and the horses are an option if anyone wants half an hour in the saddle.
- Skip if it rains or clouds sit on the pass: the whole point is the view.

## Alternatives for the afternoon (pick by mood and weather)
- **A. Cheile Râșnoavei, the gorge behind Râșnov (Tal's kind of place, optional):** a limestone gorge 10 min from the hotel with a guided via ferrata (450-750 m long, 250 m of climbing, 2 hours, medium-hard grade, 200 lei (€40) plus VAT per person with guide and gear from Jump Adventure, minimum age usually 14-16, book by phone the day before), ziplines and a giant swing run by Cheile Râșnoavei Adventure, and a 140 m bungee jump for the brave. Everyone else walks the gorge road (flat, 30 min) or sits at the café. Fits 16:00-18:00 instead of Fundata and Șirnea. Book only if Tal wants it.
- **B. Zărnești Gorge today instead of Monday:** the flat gorge walk into Piatra Craiului is 10 min from the sanctuary, so 11:30-13:00 here (see [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Day 6 - Mon Sep 21 - Rasnov to Bucharest via the Brasov area\|Day 6 - Mon Sep 21 - Rasnov to Bucharest via the Brasov area]]) and lunch in Zărnești, then Măgura in the afternoon. Monday then gets the Brasov old town morning or the Poiana Brașov cable car instead.
- **C. Hărman and Prejmer fortified churches:** the Saxon churches inside rings of walls, 10 and 18 km north-east of Brasov. Hărman is open Sunday 10:00-18:00, 10 lei (€2), 30 min; Prejmer, the big UNESCO one, is **closed on Sundays**. 40 min drive each way from Bran, so only instead of the pass, and the pass is the better afternoon.
- **D. Râșnov fortress hill:** the fortress interior is closed for renovation until 2028, but the 20 min walk up from the Dino Parc car park gives the view over Râșnov, Bucegi and Piatra Craiului. A filler on the way home, 45 min.
- **E. Brasov old town in daylight:** Black Church interior (15 lei (€3)), Council Square, the White Tower path, Șchei quarter, 2 hours before dinner instead of Fundata. Only if Saturday evening was too short.
- **F. A wild bear hide after all:** if the family wants wild bears as well, hides at the edge of Zărnești are 15 min from the hotel, run by the local forest manager, tours from Brasov at 15:00-16:00, about 4 hours, roughly 85 percent sighting rate, around 400 lei (€80) per person. Not planned; listed for completeness.

## Useful to know
- Sunday in the Bran area: restaurants fill up at 13:00-14:00 with day-trippers from Bucharest; book Măgura.
- The Măgura and Șirnea roads are gravel; fine in a normal car when dry, slow after rain.
- Fuel in Râșnov or Brasov; nothing on the pass.
- Warm layer for Fundata at 1,360 m in the late afternoon, and for the bear enclosures in the morning shade.
