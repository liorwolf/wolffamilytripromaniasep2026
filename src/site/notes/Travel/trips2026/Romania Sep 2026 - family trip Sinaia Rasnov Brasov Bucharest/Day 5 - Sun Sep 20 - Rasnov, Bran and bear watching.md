# Day 5 - Sun Sep 20 - Rasnov, Bran and bear watching

Bears twice: the **Libearty bear sanctuary** at Zărnești at 10:00 (Aya bought the tickets on 15 Sep), then the high villages and passes around Bran, and the wild bear hide at 17:30 if that booking still stands. **Bran castle is seen from the outside only** (Aya's choice, 15 Sep): a photo stop, no tickets, no queue, which frees the middle of the day for the countryside. The sanctuary is 20 min from the hotel, so we leave at 09:20, the only day with an earlier start than 10:00. Be at the gate 15 min before the tour.

## Stops in detail

### Libearty bear sanctuary, Zărnești (booked)
- **Worth it: 5/5.** Run by the charity Milioane de Prieteni (Millions of Friends): 69 hectares of oak forest at the edge of Zărnești holding about 100 brown bears rescued from cages, circuses and restaurants, plus a few wolves and deer. The Classic Tour is a guided walk of about 1 h 15 on a 2 km forest trail past the big fenced enclosures, with the bears a few metres away in the trees, pools and meadows. Not wild, but guaranteed bears, and the guide tells each animal's story.
- **Tickets: bought by Aya (15 Sep) on millionsoffriends.org for Sunday 20 Sep, 10:00 tour.** Prices that day: adult 100 lei (€20), student 80 lei (€16), child 55 lei (€11). Show the e-tickets on the phone at the gate. Tours run Tuesday to Sunday in the morning only, so the bears are not disturbed. Quiet voices on the trail, no feeding, no flash.
- Getting there: 20 min from the Cristian hotel via Râșnov to Zărnești, then the signposted road to the sanctuary gate (Strada Tohanul Vechi, free parking). Toilets and a small shop at the entrance. Shoes for a forest path.

### Bran castle, outside only
- **Worth it: 4/5 from outside, which is where it looks best.** The 14th-century castle sits on a rock above the village with its towers and red roofs; the famous photographs are all taken from below. Inside it is Queen Marie's furnished 1920s house, small rooms and a lot of stairs, and on a Sunday at noon it is packed. Skipping it saves 100 lei (€20) a head and an hour and a half.
- Where to look from: the meadow at the foot of the rock (walk through the souvenir market from the car park, 5 min), the path up to the gate (free up to the ticket booth), and, best of all, the open fields at the start of the Măgura road 300 m south of the castle, where you see the whole castle above the village with Bucegi behind. 20 min in total.
- If anyone changes their mind on the spot: tickets at the gate, about 100 lei (€20) adults, 40 lei (€8) for Tal (5-17), 60 lei (€12) students with a card; expect a 20-30 min queue at noon on a Sunday. Open until 18:00.

### Măgura and Peștera villages
- **Worth it: 5/5 for the setting, and this is the "villages where people still live the old way" line of the original plan, done properly.** Two scattered villages on a high plateau at 1,000 m between the Piatra Craiului wall and the Bucegi, wooden farms, cows, hay drying on poles, zero traffic. In late September the meadows are golden.
- Driving: from Bran centre take the road signposted Măgura (Strada Măgura), 6 km, gravel for the last part, fine in a normal car, 20 min. Park at the village church or by the pension you eat at.
- Walking: the marked path between Măgura and Peștera is 3 km each way on dirt tracks with 150 m of ups and downs; walk as far as you like and turn around, 1 h 15 is a good full version. Wear proper shoes, there are cows and mud. Views the whole way.
- Peștera has a small cave (Peștera Liliecilor, the bat cave, with a chapel built into the entrance) 20 min on foot from the village if anyone wants a cave.
- Lunch: Pensiunea Casa Zimbrilor (terrace facing Piatra Craiului, home cooking) or Hanul Măgura; both need a Sunday booking. Cash safer.

#### For Lior during the Măgura hours: Bran to Măgura on foot, or the waterfall
- The family drives up to Măgura; you run there from Bran and meet them at the village. Trail marked with a yellow cross from the Vama Medievală Bran museum, opposite the castle car park: 8 km, 450 m of climbing on forest paths and dirt roads with views back onto the castle, 3-4 hours at a walking pace, 1 h 15 at yours. Leave Bran 12:00, in Măgura 13:15, join the end of the walk and lunch at 13:50.
- Bigger: the **Moara Dracului (Devil's Mill) waterfall** from Șimon village, 5 min from Bran: 11 km round trip, 500 m up, 3 to 3.5 hours walking, 1 h 45 running. Drop-off in Șimon at 11:50, back at 13:35, the family collects you on the way down from Măgura at 15:05. Wild and quiet, into the west face of the Bucegi; the waterfall at 1,575 m is small in September but the valley is the point. Sandwiches in the car instead of lunch.

### Bear watching (Medveles, Harghita county)
- **Worth it: 5/5 if bears show up, which at the Harghita hides is close to certain.** You sit silently in a wooden hide at the forest edge while brown bears come to a feeding spot in the last two hours of daylight, often a mother with cubs. Bring binoculars and a long lens, no flash, quiet clothes, warm layers (it gets cold sitting still).
- The Medveles hides are near Băile Tușnad and Gheorgheni, not near Brasov. From Cristian, the Băile Tușnad hide is about 1 h 30 via Brasov and Sfântu Gheorghe (DN11 to Chichiș, then DN12, good road). The Gheorgheni hide is 3 hours, in which case the day needs re-planning. **Check the booking confirmation for the hide location and the meeting point.**
- The program starts two hours before sunset (sunset 19:20, so meet about 17:00-17:15) and ends after dark, about 19:45. Then 1 h 30 back in the dark on a good road.
- Everyone stays silent for an hour or more in the hide; phones on silent, no rustling jackets.
- **On the way, if we are 40 min ahead:** the **Hărman fortified church**, right on DN11 10 km past Brasov, a 13th-century Saxon church inside a ring of walls with storerooms, open Sunday 10:00-18:00, 10 lei (€2), 30 min. Prejmer, the bigger UNESCO one 10 min further, is closed on Sundays.

## Alternatives for the free hours (pick by mood and weather)
- **A. Rucăr-Bran pass, Fundata and Șirnea (the big-view drive, 2.5 hours):** from Bran take DN73 south through Moieciu to **Fundata** (1,360 m, the highest village in Romania, 25 min), the pass itself with the Bucegi on one side and Piatra Craiului on the other, then the side road to **Șirnea** (Romania's first "tourist village", 1968, scattered farms on a ridge, 10 min). Walk 45 min along the ridge road in Șirnea, buy smoked cheese and cheese in fir bark (brânză de burduf) from a farm or the Fundata shops, coffee at the Cheile Grădiștei resort terrace (horse riding, carts and ATVs for hire if anyone wants). Back to Bran in 30 min. Fits 14:45-17:30 on the relaxed day, or replaces the Măgura walk (12:15-14:45, lunch at Fundata Restaurant at Cheile Grădiștei) on the bear day.
- **B. Cheile Râșnoavei, the gorge behind Râșnov (Tal's kind of place, optional):** a limestone gorge 10 min from the hotel with a via ferrata (450-750 m long, 250 m of climbing, 2 hours, medium-hard grade, 200 lei (€40) plus VAT per person with guide and gear from Jump Adventure, minimum age usually 14-16, book by phone the day before), ziplines and a giant swing run by Cheile Râșnoavei Adventure, and a 140 m bungee jump for the brave. Everyone else walks the gorge road (flat, 30 min) or sits at the café. Fits 16:00-18:00 on the relaxed day. Book only if Tal wants it.
- **C. Zărnești Gorge today instead of Monday:** the flat gorge walk into Piatra Craiului is 10 min from the sanctuary, so 11:30-13:00 here (see [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Day 6 - Mon Sep 21 - Rasnov to Bucharest via the Brasov area\|Day 6 - Mon Sep 21 - Rasnov to Bucharest via the Brasov area]]) and lunch in Zărnești, then Măgura in the afternoon. Monday then gets the Brasov old town morning or the Poiana Brașov cable car instead.
- **D. Râșnov fortress hill:** the fortress interior is closed for renovation until 2028, but the 20 min walk up from the Dino Parc car park gives the view over Râșnov, Bucegi and Piatra Craiului. A filler on the way home, 45 min.
- **E. Brasov old town in daylight:** Black Church interior (15 lei (€3)), Council Square, the White Tower path, Șchei quarter, 2 hours. Only if Saturday evening was too short; the bear road goes through Brasov anyway.

## Useful to know
- Sunday in the Bran area: restaurants fill up at 13:00-14:00 with day-trippers from Bucharest; book Măgura.
- Fuel up in Brasov on the way to the bears; there is little after Sfântu Gheorghe.
- Take passports or ID copies in the car, the bear guides sometimes register names.
- The Măgura and Șirnea roads are gravel; fine in a normal car when dry, slow after rain.
