## Stops in detail

### Getting to the hotel (no car tonight)
- **Walk.** The Hilton Garden Inn is on the airport side of Calea Bucureștilor, next to the Vienna House Easy hotel, about 150 m from the departures terminal and 10-15 min from the arrivals exit with luggage. Signposted most of the way; the pavement is uneven in places, so lift the suitcases over the kerbs. Guests describe it as "5-6 min walking from the airport". If unsure, ask at the information desk in the arrivals hall which exit is nearest the hotels.
- **Bolt or Uber** (card in the app, no cash, no negotiation, a minimum fare of roughly 10-15 lei (€2-3) for a ride this short). The ride-hailing pickup is on the right side of the parking platform as you exit the arrivals building on the first floor; the app shows the current point because the airport is being rebuilt and the pickup moves.
- **Kiosk taxi.** Yellow touch-screen terminals stand on both sides of the exit door after baggage claim. Choose Meridian or Cristaxi, take the printed slip with the taxi number, go to the marked rank outside. Metered at about 3 lei (€0.60) per km, so 15-20 lei (€3-4) to the hotel; pay in lei cash and round up to 20-25 lei. Drivers can be grumpy about a 3 km ride, but the kiosk assigns the car, so nobody has to negotiate. Never take a taxi that approaches you in the hall.
- **Hotel shuttle.** The hotel lists a shuttle, but it is on request, some guests were quoted about 90 lei (€18) each way, and it may need booking a day ahead. Call reception at +40 31 403 5600 after landing if the walk is not wanted; do not count on it.

### Lei (RON)
- Draw **400-600 lei (€80-120)** at a **bank** ATM in the arrivals hall, straight after the sliding doors past baggage claim: machines on the left near the Segafredo café and more near the information desk. Raiffeisen, Banca Transilvania, BCR and BRD are fine; **skip Euronet** (high fee, poor rate). When the machine offers to convert to euros or shekels, **decline and choose lei** so our bank does the conversion.
- The exchange booths in the terminal have bad rates; do not use them.
- Cards work at the hotel and the restaurant. Cash is for the kiosk taxi (cash only), tips, and the cash-only stops later in the trip (Seven Ladders Canyon, market food, small car parks).
- Roughly 5 lei to the euro, 4.3 lei to the dollar.

### Dinner tonight: Casa Românească, Otopeni
- **Calea Bucureștilor 285A**, the building next to the hotel (the hotel is number 283), 2-3 min on foot. Phone **+40 744 689 920**. Open **10:00-24:00 daily**, so a 22:45 arrival works; call first so the kitchen holds for us.
- Traditional Romanian food and grill: ciorbă (sour soup), sarmale (stuffed cabbage), mici (grilled meat rolls), grilled trout, papanași for dessert. Terrace, full bar, sometimes live music. Rated first of the Otopeni restaurants on Tripadvisor (3.9/5, 211 reviews); reviews praise the food and the atmosphere, a few complain about the bill, so check it before paying. About 60-90 lei (€12-18) per person with a drink.
- **Backups if it is full or closing:** the hotel's Wright Flyer restaurant (dinner until about 23:00, ask at check-in; room service is only 17:00-21:00) and the 24-hour pantry in the lobby for snacks; Glovo or Bolt Food delivery to the hotel lobby; the 24-hour Carrefour in the domestic arrivals hall of the terminal for sandwiches before leaving the airport. Airport Grill on the boulevard closes at 17:00, and The Bull steakhouse in the Vienna House next door closes at 22:00, so neither works tonight.

### Otopeni airport tomorrow morning: the rental car
- The desk is closed tonight, so Lior (and one more driver if two are named on the contract) walks back to the terminal at about 08:45, picks up the car at 09:00, and brings it to the hotel car park by 09:45. Everyone else has breakfast and is in the lobby with the bags at 10:00.
- Rental desks are in the arrivals hall; the cars are in a car park a few minutes' walk or a shuttle away. Take photos of every scratch and of the fuel gauge before leaving.
- Ask the desk for a **rovinietă** (the electronic road vignette). Rentals almost always include it, but driving national roads without one is a fine.
- Buy water for the car. There is a Mega Image supermarket at Calea Bucureștilor 178-180, 3 min by car from the hotel, on the way out.

### Hilton Garden Inn Bucharest Airport
- Modern, quiet, on the airport side of the DN1 road, so the morning drive to Sinaia starts straight onto the highway with no city traffic. Reception 24 hours, free parking.
- Restaurant open late for a snack, and a 24-hour pantry in the lobby.
- **Gym:** 24 hours, Technogym cardio and weights. **Breakfast:** 06:00-10:30, buffet plus cooked to order; included only if the rate says so, otherwise about 75-90 lei (€15-18) per person. Eat by 09:30 to leave at 10:00.

## Useful to know
- Time zone is the same as Israel.
- Lei (RON): roughly 5 lei to the euro, 4.3 lei to the dollar. Tipping 10 percent in restaurants.
- Driving: right-hand traffic, headlights on at all times outside towns, 50 km/h in towns, 90 on national roads, 130 on the motorway. Speed cameras are common on DN1.
