### Extra hotel facts
- Hilton Garden Inn: free parking, restaurant open for dinner until 23:00, 24-hour pantry shop, 3 km from the terminal, free airport shuttle on request.
- Hotel Sinaia: the spa is the treat of the trip for Aya and Tal; Friday 17:00-18:00 after the mountain is free, 18:00-20:00 costs 20 lei (€4). Towels provided. Check-in from 15:00, check-out 12:00.
- Vila Retezat: in the Furnica quarter, a steep 10 minute drive above the town, quiet, panoramic. Free parking. Check-in from 14:00.
- Hotel Sorin Mihai: check-in from 14:00, check-out 12:00, free parking, EV charger, restaurant à la carte for lunch and dinner. It is in Cristian, not Rasnov: 8 km to Rasnov, 10 km to Brasov, 20 km to Bran.
- Concorde Old Bucharest: check-in from 15:00, check-out 12:00 (we need the car and the parking spot until 17:30 on Tuesday, ask at check-in). Parking is reserved (Aya), about 140 lei (€28) per day. The old-town approach is via Splaiul Independenței and Strada Șelari; ask the hotel for the exact route.

## Notes
- Sinaia: the group is split between two places, Hotel Sinaia and Vila Retezat, 10 minutes apart by car. Breakfast times differ (07:00 vs 08:30), so the 10:00 departure is from Hotel Sinaia after picking up the Retezat sleepers at 09:50.
- Cristian is the base for the Brasov area days (Bran, Măgura, Zărnești, Seven Ladders).
- Lior's gym mornings: Hilton (Thu), Hotel Sinaia spa gym (Fri and Sat, book 09:00), Concorde (Tue). No gym Sun and Mon in Cristian: run instead (the Cristian to Râșnov cycle path is flat, 8 km each way; or the Râșnov fortress hill, 2 km and 150 m up from the Dino Parc car park).
