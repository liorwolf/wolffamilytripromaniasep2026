# Day 4 - Sat Sep 19 - Sinaia to Rasnov

Moving day, but every stop is on the direct road north, so there is no backtracking: a waterfall walk from Bușteni, the Seven Ladders Canyon with its ladders and zipline, and check-in in Cristian (the hotel "in Rasnov" is actually in Cristian, between Rasnov and Brasov).

## Timeline (leaving the hotel at 10:00)
| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 10:00 | Check out of both Sinaia hotels, luggage in the car. Drive 10 min to Bușteni. |
| 10:15 | **Cascada Urlătoarea** walk from the Bușteni cable car car park: 45 min each way through forest, flat then a gentle climb. |
| 12:00 | Back at the car. Drive DN1 north over Predeal pass to Timișu de Jos, 25 km, 40 min. |
| 12:45 | **Seven Ladders Canyon (Canionul Șapte Scări).** Park in the village, walk 45-60 min up the forest road, ticket cabin, the canyon on metal ladders, zipline option down. About 3 hours total. Sandwiches from a Bușteni bakery on the way. |
| 16:00 | Drive 30 min to Cristian. |
| 16:30 | Check in at Hotel Sorin Mihai (Strada Lungă 215, Cristian), 3 double rooms, free parking. |
| 17:30 | Brasov old town, 15 min drive: Council Square, the Black Church from outside (closes 17:00 Sat), Strada Sforii (one of Europe's narrowest streets), Catherine's Gate. Dinner in the old town. |
| 21:00 | Back to Cristian. |

## Stops in detail

### Cascada Urlătoarea, Bușteni
- **Worth it: 3/5.** A 15 m waterfall in a forest gorge under the Jepii cliffs, popular with local families. Nothing dramatic, but a pleasant, shaded warm-up walk and a nice photo.
- Walking: 5 km round trip, 100 m up, 1 h 30 with the stop. Start from the Bușteni cable car car park (paid, 10 lei (€2)), follow the wide path marked with a yellow triangle. Small kiosks at the start.
- Skip it if you want more time in Brasov later, or if yesterday was long.

### Seven Ladders Canyon
- **Worth it: 5/5 for Tal (a climber's canyon), Guy and Noa, 4/5 for the rest.** A narrow limestone canyon in the Piatra Mare mountains climbed on seven steel ladders bolted next to seven waterfalls, the highest ladder 15 m. Wet, loud, fun. At the top there is a **zipline** back down over the forest in several sections (about 50 lei (€10), 2 km of cable, the longest zipline circuit in Romania).
- Practical: hours 10:00-18:00, last entry to the canyon 17:30, last zipline 17:00. Entry about 10 lei (€2) per adult, 5 lei (€1) for pupils and students with ID (Tal, Guy and Noa), **cash only**. Saturday afternoon is busy; going up at 13:00 rather than 11:00 avoids the morning tour groups.
- Walking: park in Timișu de Jos (follow the "Canionul Șapte Scări" signs off DN1, 300 m into the village, roadside parking, free or 10 lei (€2)). Then a forest road of 45-60 min, 250 m up, easy but steady, to the ticket cabin. The canyon itself is 20 min of ladders. Return the same way (or by zipline). Total 2.5 to 3 hours.
- Shoes with grip, the ladders and rock are wet. Small backpack, both hands free. Not for anyone afraid of heights, although the ladders have cages on the exposed parts. Aya and Tal can wait at the canyon entrance kiosk (drinks, benches) if they prefer not to climb, or walk back down slowly.

#### For Lior: tennis with a coach at the hotel (Saturday 17:00 or Sunday 08:00)
- The hotel runs its own **Tenis Club Sorin Mihai**: three standard clay courts at the entrance of Cristian on the DN73 (Strada Voluntari 35, a few hundred metres from the rooms), with changing rooms, showers, a small shop, racquet stringing and a bar terrace, and a view of Postăvarul, Bucegi and Piatra Craiului. The club has qualified coaches for beginner and advanced adults (Adrian Badu is the coach named on the club page).
- **How to book a lesson:** phone or WhatsApp the club and hotel number **0754 657 648** (contact Crina Toacse) or email rezervari@hotelsorinmihai.ro, in English is fine; ask for "o oră cu antrenor" (one hour with a coach) plus a lent racquet ("rachetă de împrumut") and balls. Do it this week, not on arrival: a coach on a Saturday evening or Sunday morning needs notice. Prices are not published; Brasov coaches charge about 100-150 lei (€20-30) per hour plus the court at about 50-70 lei (€10-14), so budget 200 lei (€40) for the hour.
- Slots that fit the plan: **Saturday 17:00-18:00**, right after check-in and before Brasov (then dinner in Brasov at 19:30), or **Sunday 08:00-09:00** before the 10:00 departure to Bran (breakfast 08:00-10:00, so eat after). The club also has a free open-door session on Mondays 17:00-18:00 for adults, which we miss.
- Fallbacks in Brasov if the hotel coach is not available: **JoacăTenis** (Bulevardul Gării 5, next to the Sala Sporturilor, Mon-Sat 08:00-21:00, individual lessons for adults, racquets provided, phone +40 724 654 397, joacatenisbrasov@gmail.com, English site), **Optim Tennis Club** (coach Alex Stan, GPTCA-certified, optimtennis.ro), **One Tennis Academy** (Carpatex complex near AFI mall). All 15 min from Cristian.

#### For Lior: Piatra Mare peak from the top of the canyon
- After the ladders, the red-stripe trail continues from the canyon exit up to Cabana Piatra Mare and **Piatra Mare peak** (1,843 m): 4 km and 550 m of climbing beyond the canyon, 1 h up at your pace and 1 h 15 back down the forest road to the car. Leaving the canyon top at 14:00 gets you to the summit at 15:00 and to the car at 16:15. The family goes down by zipline or on foot by 15:30 and waits at the village kiosk in Timișu de Jos (drinks, benches), or drives the 30 min to Cristian to check in and Aya comes back for you (or you take a Bolt, 40 lei (€8)). Views from the summit: Brasov, Bucegi, Piatra Craiului.
- Shorter: run the forest road up to the canyon entrance in 25 min instead of walking it in 50 while the family strolls, then wait at the ticket cabin and climb the ladders together.

### Hotel Sorin Mihai, Cristian
- Family-run hotel on the main road of Cristian, 8 km from Rasnov and 10 km from Brasov. Restaurant with Romanian food, free parking, quiet.
- **Gym:** none. Three clay tennis courts on site instead (Tenis Club Sorin Mihai, see the tennis section below); anyone can rent a court and racquets this evening or Sunday morning. **Breakfast:** 08:00-10:00, buffet, charged separately unless our rate includes it, about 40-50 lei (€8-10) per person. Ask tonight for a 07:30 start on Monday and a packed lunch for the gorge. See [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Hotels and sleeping\|Hotels and sleeping]].
- Cristian itself has a fortified Saxon church (1500s) worth a 10 min look.

### Brasov old town, evening
- **Worth it: 4/5.** The Saxon medieval centre: Council Square (Piața Sfatului) with the Council House, the Black Church (Romania's largest Gothic church, blackened by the 1689 fire), the White and Black towers on the hill, Strada Sforii, Catherine's Gate and Șchei gate.
- Park at the Piața Sfatului underground car park or Livada Poștei (3-4 lei (€0.6-0.8) per hour, card).
- Dinner: Sergiana (traditional, cellar, book), La Ceaun (stews from cast-iron pots, casual), Bistro de l'Arte (relaxed), Prato (Italian). Ice cream and the fountain in the square after.

#### Optional for Tal: indoor climbing in Brasov
- Only if Tal wants it; nothing else in the evening depends on it. Brasov has two gyms. **Climb House Brasov** (Strada Panselelor, inside the Metrom industrial park, gate 2; big modern bouldering and rope hall, 10 min drive from the old town) and **Natural High** (Strada Carpaților 60, smaller and more central, phone 0746 803 932). Day passes are roughly 40-60 lei (€8-12) with shoe rental 15-20 lei (€3-4); phone or check their Facebook pages for Saturday hours, most Romanian gyms run until 21:00-22:00. If she goes: 17:30-19:00 at the gym (bouldering needs no partner; for the rope wall ask the gym about a belay or an instructor), whoever wants to join goes along, the others walk the old town, everyone meets for dinner at 19:30. Book Sergiana or La Ceaun either way.

#### For Lior in Brasov: Tâmpa on foot
- While the others walk the square and pick a restaurant, run the Serpentine path up **Tâmpa** (960 m) from behind the Black Church: 2.5 km and 400 m of climbing, 25-30 min up at your pace, 20 min down. The BRASOV sign, the whole old town below, sunset at 19:15. Back at the square for dinner at 19:30. The path is well made and lit on the lower part; the cable car (45 lei (€9) return) closes at 17:00 on Saturday, so this is the way up anyway.

## Alternatives we might like more
- **Dino Parc Râșnov instead of the waterfall:** a dinosaur park with 100 life-size models in the forest under Râșnov fortress, a 9D cinema and playgrounds, open daily 09:30-20:00, about 28 lei (€5.5) adults, 22 lei (€4.5) kids. A children's park; with Tal at 15 and Guy and Noa at 25 it is 2/5 at best, listed only because it is next to the hotel. It is 10 min from the hotel, so it also fits any spare hour on Sunday or Monday.
- **Tâmpa cable car in Brasov:** instead of the waterfall, go straight to Seven Ladders at 11:00, be in Cristian by 15:00, then Brasov: the Tâmpa cable car (45 lei (€9) return, 3 min, Sat 09:30-17:00) to the Hollywood-style BRASOV sign and the view over the old town, walk down the zigzag path in 45 min. Then dinner. Note the cable car does not run on Mondays, so Saturday is the day for it.
- **Rain day:** Seven Ladders is fine in light rain but not in a storm (the waterfalls swell). Rain plan: Cantacuzino Castle interior in Bușteni, then Brasov's Black Church interior and the First Romanian School museum in Șchei, then the Râșnov fortress panorama (the fortress is closed for renovation until 2028, but the terrace and the hill are open).

## Useful to know
- Predeal pass on DN1 is the highest point of the road (1,040 m); Saturday traffic towards Brasov is heavy after 15:00, so being on the road at 12:00 is good.
- There is a bakery and a Profi supermarket on Bușteni's main street for picnic food.
- Tomorrow is Sunday with the bear watching at 17:30, 1.5 hours away: the afternoon is fixed, so tonight is the evening to sleep early.
