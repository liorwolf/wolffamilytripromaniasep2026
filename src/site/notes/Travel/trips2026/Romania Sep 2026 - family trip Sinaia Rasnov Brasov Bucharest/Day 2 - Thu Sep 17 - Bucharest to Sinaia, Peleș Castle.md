# Day 2 - Thu Sep 17 - Bucharest to Sinaia, Peleș Castle

Leave the airport hotel at 10:00, drive up the Prahova valley, see the best castle in Romania in the early afternoon, then walk straight from the castle car park into the mountains. Sleep in Sinaia.

## Stops in detail

### The drive, Bucharest to Sinaia
- 125 km, 2 h to 2 h 30 depending on DN1 traffic. Leaving at 10:00 on a Thursday is after the morning rush; Ploiești's ring road is the slow part.
- Fuel at Ploiești or Comarnic. The valley road from Comarnic to Sinaia is two lanes and slow behind trucks; relax, it is 30 min.
- Good stop if anyone needs it: the Peco or Lukoil stations after Ploiești have clean toilets and coffee.

### Peleș Castle
- **Worth it: 5/5.** A late 19th-century royal summer palace, 160 rooms of carved wood, stained glass, weapons hall, the first castle in Europe with electricity. Everyone in the family will like it, and the guided tour keeps it to about an hour.
- **Booking is required.** Since 2025 tickets are limited to 2,000 a day, sold for time slots, online at bilete.peles.ro or at the machines by the castle. Thursday slots: 09:15-11:00, 11:00-12:45, 12:45-14:30, 14:30-16:00. Book the **12:45-14:30** slot now for 5 people; slots cannot be changed later. Open Wed 10:00-17:00, Thu-Sun 09:15-17:00, closed Mon and Tue.
- Price: 100 lei (€20) per adult for ground floor plus first floor. **Pupils and students pay 25 lei (€5)** with a valid student ID, pensioners 50 lei (€10). The museum lists no nationality rule, so Tal (15) should qualify as a pupil: bring her school ID card with photo plus passport, and pick the pupil ticket when booking online; if the ticket office rejects the Israeli card, the difference is 75 lei (€15). Guy and Noa (25) are graduate students: with their university student cards they also get the 25 lei (€5) student ticket, so the five of us could pay 100 + 100 + 25 + 25 + 25 lei (€55) instead of 500 lei (€100). Book the student and pupil tickets online and carry the cards. The second-floor royal apartments cost extra and add 30 min; skip them unless someone is keen. Photo permit extra.
- Walking: 400 m uphill from the car park to the castle, 10 min, on cobbles.
- Lunch before: the terraces along Strada Peleșului serve grilled meats, soups and pizza; not great, but fast. Better and 5 min away: Bucegi restaurant on the main road, or Snow Restaurant.

#### For Lior during the castle (12:45-14:15)
- The family's guided hour inside is your window. From the Peleș car park take the red-triangle path straight up to **Poiana Stânii Regale** (1,270 m): 4 km and 490 m of climbing one way, 40-45 min up at your pace (Google's 1 h 10 is a walking pace) and 30 min down. You are back at the car by 14:15 and then go up a second time with the family, on foot at their speed or in the car on the forest road, or run ahead and wait for them at the hut.
- Longer version if you skip the second round: from Poiana Stânii continue to **Cota 1400** (200 m more, 30 min fast), coffee at the terrace, then down the Șipot path into Sinaia centre (40 min), a 12 km loop with 700 m up, back in town by 15:00 where the family finds you after the castle and Pelișor.

### Poiana Stânii Regale and the Franz Joseph cliffs: walk or drive
- **Worth it: 4/5.** The royal family's picnic meadow at 1,270 m above Sinaia, with a small hut (Cabana Stâna Regală, drinks and simple food when open) and, 5 min along the forest road, the Franz Joseph rocks: railed platforms on the cliff edge with the view over the whole Prahova valley and Bușteni. King Carol I named the main rock after the Austrian emperor's visit.
- **On foot, the Royal Path (Poteca Regală):** starts at the Peleș car park, marked red triangle, through beech and fir forest. 4 km and 490 m of climbing one way; Google says 1 h 10 up at a normal pace, allow 1 h 30 with the family and 50 min down. Good path, steep in the middle third.
- **By car (Aya's find):** a gravel forest road branches right off the Sinaia to Cota 1400 road at about 1,020 m, just after the Mountain Gendarmerie training centre, and climbs 3.5 km to the meadow. 23 min from the castle. The hut administrators say any normal car can do it; it is unpaved and can be rutted after rain. **Decide on the spot:** drive up to the junction, look at the surface, and if it is fine everyone drives; if not, the car goes back to the hotel and the walkers take the path.
- Mixed version that gets everyone the view: Aya drives Tal and whoever is tired up the forest road and parks at the meadow; the walkers go up the Royal Path and meet them at the hut; everyone walks the 5 min to the rocks together; the walkers go down on foot, the others by car. Total time about 2 hours instead of 3.
- Sunset is 19:20, so a 14:30 start leaves plenty of margin either way.

#### For Lior during the castle (12:45-14:15)
- The family's guided hour inside is your window. From the Peleș car park take the red-triangle path straight up to **Poiana Stânii Regale** (1,270 m): 4 km and 490 m of climbing one way, 40-45 min up at your pace and 30 min down. You are back at the car by 14:15 and then walk the Royal Path a second time with the family at their speed, or run ahead to the Franz Joseph cliffs and wait for them at the meadow.
- Longer version if you skip the second round: from Poiana Stânii continue to **Cota 1400** (200 m more, 30 min fast), coffee at the terrace, then down the Șipot path into Sinaia centre (40 min), a 12 km loop with 700 m up, back in town by 15:00 where the family finds you after the castle and Pelișor.

### Royal Path to the Franz Joseph cliffs
- **Worth it: 4/5 for the walkers, 2/5 for anyone who dislikes climbing.** The original itinerary calls this a 24 min drive, it is not: it is a hike that starts right at the Peleș car park, marked with a red triangle, through beech and fir forest to the meadow of Poiana Stânii Regale (1,270 m) and on to the rock ledges of Franz Joseph with the view over the whole Prahova valley and Bușteni.
- Walking: 8 km round trip, 490 m up, about 3 hours including the viewpoint. Good path, steep in the middle third. Water only from what you carry.
- Shorter version for Aya and Tal: walk 50 min to Poiana Stânii (the meadow with the small terrace, sometimes open), enjoy the view, walk back, 2 hours total. Or skip and do the castles version below.
- Sunset is 19:20, so a 14:30 start leaves plenty of margin.

### Sinaia town in the evening
- Dimitrie Ghica park and the casino (1913, tours only by arrangement, lovely from outside), the monastery (1695, two courtyards, the old church has original frescoes, free to enter, museum 10:00-16:00), the Belle Époque villas on the main street.
- Dinner: Snow Restaurant (Romanian, good ciorbă and sarmale, terrace), Ramayana (Indian, surprisingly good), Taverna Sârbului (Serbian grill, big portions, 10 min drive towards Bușteni, popular with families, book).

#### For Lior in the evening
- While the others stroll the park and the casino, run the flat riverside path along the Prahova from Sinaia towards Bușteni and back: 8-10 km in 45 min, no climbing, back for dinner at 19:30. Or the asphalt road from the town up to Cota 1400 (10 km one way, 600 m up) as a hard 1 hour uphill run and 35 min back down, in daylight until 19:20.

## Alternatives we might like more
- **Castles instead of the hike, for Aya and Tal:** book Pelișor for 14:30 (separate ticket, about 50 lei (€10), 40 min, Queen Marie's Art Nouveau house, the Golden Room lined in gold leaf; closed from 28 Sep, so we are in time). Then the town and a coffee at the casino terrace. The others meet them at 17:30.
- **Forest road to Poiana Stânii by car** (see above) is the zero-effort way to the same viewpoint as the hike, and the better choice than Cota 1400 below if the road is dry.
- **Cota 1400 by car for the view:** if legs are tired after the flight, drive the 10 km mountain road from Sinaia to Cota 1400 (20 min), walk 30 min on the plateau paths, see the valley from above, drive down. Zero effort, 3/5 views.
- **Earlier castle, later hike:** if the 12:45 slot is sold out, take 14:30-16:00 and do a shorter Poiana Stânii walk from 11:30 first, then lunch at 13:30, then the castle.

- Both hotels are 6 min from the castle: Hotel Sinaia (booked as Rina Sinaia, family room) and Vila Retezat Sinaia (triple), 10 min apart.
- **Hotel Sinaia:** gym inside the Silena Mountain Spa with pool, jacuzzi and saunas, free 09:00-18:00 by reservation at reception, 20 lei (€4) per adult 18:00-20:00. Book a spa slot for tomorrow evening at check-in. Breakfast 07:00-10:00, buffet, normally included in the room rate (check the confirmation).
- **Vila Retezat:** no gym, small pool and hot tub in the garden. Breakfast 08:30-10:00, continental, included. See [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Hotels and sleeping\|Hotels and sleeping]].

- Pack for tomorrow: layers, hat, gloves, rain shell, 1.5 l water per person, snacks. The plateau is 0-10 C in the wind even in September.
