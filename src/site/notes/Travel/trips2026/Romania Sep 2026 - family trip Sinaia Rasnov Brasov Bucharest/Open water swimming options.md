# Open water swimming options

Honest summary, checked 13 Sep 2026: the Carpathian part of the trip has almost no swimmable open water in late September. The mountains have cold reservoirs, the Brasov lakes are dirty, and the one famous lake near the bear hide forbids swimming. The real open-water swim is Snagov on the last day. The rest below are dips, fallbacks and pools, listed so nothing is missed. All optional.

| Day | Water | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Fri 18 (Sinaia) | Lake Bolboci, Bucegi, 1,440 m | Cold dip only, 10-13 C. Only if the lifts are closed and we drive the Transbucegi anyway |
| Sat 19 / Mon 21 (Brasov) | Local lakes | No. Bazinul Olimpic Brasov (50 m indoor pool) is the swim workout instead |
| Sun 20 (Băile Tușnad) | Lake Sfânta Ana | Swimming forbidden. Wellness Tușnad mineral pools 08:00-22:00 if there is time before the bears |
| Tue 22 (Bucharest) | Snagov lake | Yes. 1-2 km along the shore, kayak escort, the Croatia warm-up. Already in the plan |
| Tue 22 (Bucharest) | Lacul Morii | No. Swimming forbidden and polluted |

## Sinaia: Lake Bolboci (Friday, only as a fallback)
- "The sea of the Bucegi": a 1928 hydro reservoir at 1,440 m on the Ialomița, 7 m deep at most, clear water, conifer shores, ridges above 2,000 m all around, picnic tables, Cabana Bolboci on the shore. Locals swim from the shore in July and August and kayak on it.
- Late September the water is about 10-13 C: a 5-10 minute cold dip with the swim buoy, not a distance swim. No lifeguards, nobody around on a weekday.
- Getting there: 40 min from Sinaia over the Transbucegi road (narrow switchbacks), so 1 h 20 of driving for the round trip. It is on the far side of the massif from everything else, which is why it only makes sense on **Friday's alternative B** (wind or fog closes the gondola and cable car), when the plan drives there anyway. See [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Day 3 - Fri Sep 18 - Sinaia, Bucegi and the Sphinx\|Day 3 - Fri Sep 18 - Sinaia, Bucegi and the Sphinx]].
- Not worth a detour on Saturday: the road north goes the other way.
- Pool fallback in Sinaia: Hotel Sinaia's indoor pool in the Silena spa, free 09:00-18:00 by reservation, short and warm, good for a stretch, not for laps.

## Brasov area: no lake, one good pool
- The lakes around Brasov (Codlea, Săcele, Tărlung reservoir) are swum by locals "at their own risk"; the water is not clean, there are no beaches or lifeguards, and Tărlung is a drinking-water reservoir. Skip.
- **Bazinul Olimpic "Mihai Mitrofan" Brasov:** a real 50 m, 10-lane indoor pool (plus a small warm-up pool), 15 min from the Cristian hotel, in the Brasov sports park south of the old town. Public entry 30 lei (€6) per session; the pool works in fixed sessions ("serii") of about 1.5 hours, enter up to 5 min before the session starts. It reopened on 24 Aug 2026 after the summer break; check the public sessions at bazinulolimpicbrasov.ro before going, mornings are usually 07:00-09:00 and evenings from 18:00. Cap and flip-flops required, towel and goggles your own.
- Fits: **Saturday 18:00** before dinner in Brasov (the pool is 10 min from the old town), or **Monday 07:00** before breakfast and the gorge, or instead of the gorge if it rains. See [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Day 4 - Sat Sep 19 - Sinaia to Rasnov\|Day 4 - Sat Sep 19 - Sinaia to Rasnov]] and [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Day 6 - Mon Sep 21 - Rasnov to Bucharest via the Brasov area\|Day 6 - Mon Sep 21 - Rasnov to Bucharest via the Brasov area]].

## Băile Tușnad (Sunday, before the bears)
- **Lake Sfânta Ana**, the only volcanic crater lake in Romania, 20 min above Băile Tușnad: swimming is forbidden to protect the water, boats only. Worth the look if we arrive an hour early for the bear program (it is 950 m up on a side road, entrance fee about 10 lei (€2) per person plus parking), not a swim.
- **Wellness Tușnad**, in the centre of the resort by the small Lake Ciucaș: outdoor and indoor mineral-water pools, whirlpools, saunas, open in summer 08:00-22:00, about 25-40 lei (€5-8). A warm soak, not a swim. Only if the timing before the 17:15 bear meeting allows, which it does not in the current plan (arrival 16:50).

## Bucharest
- **Snagov lake, Tuesday morning: the swim of the trip.** 35 km north of the centre, 15 min from the airport, long and calm, about 20 C in late September, Olympic kayakers and open-water swimmers train there. Rent a kayak at Port Natura or the Snagov Tur centre so someone paddles alongside, swim 1-2 km along the shore with the buoy, no lifeguards. Full timing and phone numbers in [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Day 7 - Tue Sep 22 - Bucharest, flight home\|Day 7 - Tue Sep 22 - Bucharest, flight home]] and [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Last day options - Tue Sep 22 - me and Guy\|Last day options - Tue Sep 22 - me and Guy]].
- **Lacul Morii**, the big reservoir 6 km west of the centre: swimming is forbidden and the water is polluted, despite people doing it in summer. Skip.
- **Lake Herăstrău**: no swimming, boats only. Run around it instead (7 km loop).
- **Therme București**: heated outdoor pools with a 25 m lane section in the Palm zone, fine for a few hundred metres, but it is a spa, not open water.
