# Romania family trip, Wed 16 - Tue 22 Sep 2026

With Aya, Tal, Guy and Noa. Fly El Al to Bucharest, rent a car, three nights in Sinaia, two nights in Rasnov (Brasov area), last night in Bucharest old town.

## Key dates
| Date | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Wed 16 Sep | Bucharest airport | LY 571 lands 21:10. Sleep Hilton airport |
| Thu 17 Sep | Sinaia | Drive 2 h. Peleș Castle, Franz Joseph path |
| Fri 18 Sep | Sinaia | Bucegi: Sphinx, Lake Bolboci, Cheile Tătarului, or Gondola Sinaia and hike |
| Sat 19 Sep | Sinaia to Rasnov | Cascada Șapte Izvoare (maybe), or head north early. 1 h drive to Rasnov |
| Sun 20 Sep | Rasnov / Brasov | Zărnești Cliffs, Botorog, Bran castle, caves, waterfalls. 17:30 bear watching |
| Mon 21 Sep | Brasov area to Bucharest | Seven Ladders Canyon, Solomon's Rocks etc. 3 h drive to Bucharest |
| Tue 22 Sep | Bucharest | Tal and Noa shopping (Aya invited), me and Guy at Snagov lake or Therme, old city, 16:30 Caru' cu Bere. Leave 17:45 for airport. LY 9495 20:45 |
