# Day 6 - Mon Sep 21 - Rasnov to Bucharest via the Brasov area

Last mountain morning in Piatra Craiului, then the 3 hour drive to Bucharest, arriving with light left for the old town. Monday is the wrong day for museums and cable cars around Brasov (Peleș, Pelișor and the Tâmpa cable car are closed, Bran opens only at 12:00), so a nature morning is the right call.

## Timeline (leaving the hotel at 10:00)
| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 10:00 | Check out, luggage in the car. Drive 20 min to Zărnești and on to the **Fântâna lui Botorog** car park (free, roadside, 1.5 km past Cabana Gura Râului). |
| 10:30 | Walk into the **Zărnești Gorge (Prăpăstiile Zărneștilor)**: flat gravel track between 200 m limestone walls, 45 min in, 45 min back. |

## Stops in detail

### Zărnești Gorge and the Piatra Craiului foothills
- **Worth it: 4/5 for the gorge, 5/5 if the walkers continue to Curmătura.** Prăpăstiile Zărneștilor is a 4 km limestone gorge, the entrance to Piatra Craiului national park; walls up to 200 m, climbers on them, a stream in spring, dry in September. The walk is on a flat forest road, easy for everyone.
- Walking, easy version: 45 min in to where the gorge opens out, 45 min back, 6 km, no climbing. Aya, Tal and anyone who wants a slow morning.
- Walking, walkers' version: from the gorge, the yellow-stripe path climbs through forest to **Cabana Curmătura** (1,470 m, a proper mountain hut with soup and tea), 2 to 2.5 hours from Botorog, 600 m up, then back down the Zănoaga meadow path in 1 h 30. About 11 km and 4 hours. With a 10:30 start the walkers are back at the car by 14:30, which leaves Bucharest for 17:30-18:00. Only do this if the group agrees to a later Bucharest arrival; otherwise the gorge alone.
- Parking is free at Botorog (roadside) and at Gura Râului. Cash for the hut (soup 25 lei (€5), tea 8 lei (€1.6)).

#### For Lior: Curmătura and Piatra Mică
- From Botorog through the gorge, then the yellow-stripe path to **Cabana Curmătura** (1,470 m), on to **Piatra Mică** (1,816 m) for the view along the whole Piatra Craiului ridge and across to the Bucegi, and down the Zănoaga meadows (blue triangle) back to Botorog: 12 km, about 1,000 m up and down, 5 to 6 hours at a walking pace, 2 h 45 to 3 h at yours. Start 10:30, back at the car by 13:30, exactly the departure time. The others do the gorge and coffee at Gura Râului, or drive 10 min to Zărnești for lunch and come back for you at 13:30. Tea and soup at the hut (cash).

### The drive to Bucharest
- 190 km, 3 hours in normal traffic, 4 or more when DN1 is jammed. Monday afternoon is the best time of the week for it. Leave by 13:30-14:00.
- Route: Râșnov to Predeal on DN73A (30 min, forest road), then DN1 down the Prahova valley through Bușteni, Sinaia, Comarnic, Ploiești. Stops: Comarnic for fuel; the last good coffee is at Sinaia.
- Entering Bucharest from DN1: through Otopeni and along Kiseleff boulevard past the Arch of Triumph, Victory square, then Calea Victoriei south to the old town. The old town has pedestrian zones and one-way streets; **ask the hotel by WhatsApp today for the exact driving approach and where the reserved parking is.**

### Concorde Old Bucharest and the old town
- The hotel is in the Lipscani area, everything below is on foot.
- **Old town (Centrul Vechi), 4/5 in the evening:** a grid of restored 19th-century streets full of terraces. Stavropoleos church (1724, tiny, carved stone, the loveliest church in the city), the Old Princely Court (Vlad the Impaler's palace ruins), Hanul lui Manuc (a 1808 caravanserai courtyard, now a restaurant), Pasajul Macca-Vilacrosse (glass-roofed arcade, hookah bars), Cărturești Carusel (a beautiful bookshop on five floors, free, everyone likes it). Walking: 2 km loop, 1.5 hours with stops.
- Dinner nearby: Hanul lui Manuc (Romanian, big courtyard, book), Lacrimi și Sfinți (modern Romanian, small, book), Caru' cu Bere is tomorrow. Or a 15 min taxi to Vacamuuu Steakhouse in Floreasca (see [[Travel/trips2026/Romania Sep 2026 - family trip Sinaia Rasnov Brasov Bucharest/Bucharest restaurants\|Bucharest restaurants]]) if the family wants the steak tonight and lighter Romanian tomorrow.

#### For Lior in Bucharest
- While the family strolls Lipscani and picks a terrace, run the flat city loop: old town, Cișmigiu gardens (1.5 km), down Bulevardul Unirii past the Parliament palace, **Carol Park** (the mausoleum on the hill has the best free view of the city), back through the old town, 8 km, 40 min. Or take a Bolt (10 min) to **Văcărești Nature Park**, the urban delta, and run the 5.3 km dyke loop with birds and views of the blocks, 5 km from the centre.

## Alternatives we might like more
- **Brasov old town morning instead of the gorge:** if Saturday evening in Brasov was too short, spend 10:15-12:30 walking the old town in daylight (Black Church interior, 15 lei (€3), open Mon from 10:00; the White Tower path for the view; Schei quarter), coffee on Council Square, then drive. Zero exertion, 4/5.
- **Râșnov morning for the kids:** Dino Parc (daily 09:30-20:00) plus the Râșnov fortress hill panorama (the fortress interior is closed for renovation until 2028), 10 min from the hotel, done by 12:30.
- **Postăvarul peak from Poiana Brașov:** drive 25 min to Poiana Brașov, cable car or gondola to 1,800 m (runs daily in September, about 60 lei (€12) return), 1 hour walk to the peak with views to Piatra Craiului and Bucegi, down by lift, 3 hours total. More mountain than the gorge, a bit more driving.
- **Sleep in Sinaia instead of Bucharest tonight (the bigger change):** keeps the whole day in the mountains, and the 2 hour drive to Bucharest happens Tuesday morning at 08:00 with no traffic. It costs the Bucharest evening and the hotel booking; only if the family finds the Bucharest night unattractive.

## Useful to know
- Bucharest parking on the street is by app or SMS in the centre, but we should not need it: hotel parking is reserved.
- Bucharest old town at night is safe and lively; ignore the touts for strip bars around Strada Gabroveni.
